  「合格顧問」 business, the invoice-for-everything), while the *consequences* stay real
  (the endings, the father's posture). The brief still says "exaggerated family comedy"; it

## T-02 When does 阿安 learn it is a test, not a rescue? / 阿安何時得知這是考核，而不是救人？

**Status / 狀態:** Open

### Vincent's proposal / Vincent 的提案

From `claude-review.md`: Stages 1–3 — 阿安 has doubts and then confirms that it is not a
rescue; the family tells him in stage 3. Stages 4–5 — the unreasonable demands; 阿安 grows
more and more dissatisfied and has no need to answer back so many times. He asks for Claude's
and Codex's comments.
/ 第一至三關：阿安起疑，並在第三關由家人確認這不是救人。第四、五關：不合理要求，阿安越來越不滿，
不需要每次都回應。請 Claude 與 Codex 提供意見。

### Claude's position (support, with additions) / Claude 的意見（支持，並補充）

1. **Reveal in layers, so V-01 is still a reveal.**
   - Layer 1 — "this is not a kidnapping" (family confirms it, stage 3).
   - Layer 2 — "小雨 knew" (the S4-03 text, already in the draft).
   - Layer 3 — "小雨 co-designed it, and it is a business" (V-01 / V-04, already in the draft).
   Each layer lands a different blow, so nothing is spent too early.
2. **Build the doubt in steps.**
   - Stage 1: tells only — the roster check, enemies asking 「這段好了沒有？」, 家豪's slip about
     小雨. Replace the S1-03 video call, where she is plainly fine, with a missed call or one
     ambiguous text.
   - Stage 2: the pre-printed forms (S2-04). He works the fault out himself — he repairs
     things for a living.
   - Stage 3: the family confirms it, openly.
3. **Recommended timing: confirm at the start of stage 3 (S3-01), through the invoice.** 舅舅
   says, in effect, "nobody was kidnapped — here is the 接待及安排費 line item". The kidnapping
   has a price; that is the satire. 阿安 then fights stage 3 knowing. *Alternative:* confirm at
   the end of stage 3 (S3-03/S3-04). That keeps stage 3 as a doubt stage, but the boss fight
   loses its edge.
4. **Answer "why does he keep going?" on screen.** This is the question the new structure must
   answer. My recommendation is his flaw: he still believes that passing means being accepted
   (「我還可以做得更好」), and he still loves her. Place 知夏's line 「你也可以想一想，自己想怎樣」
   (S3-04) right after the reveal — he is offered an exit and declines it, and ending 2 later
   echoes that road.
   Motivation arc: **1–2** rescue, then doubt → **3** hurt, "I'll pass anyway" → **4–5**
   patience wears thin → **6** not "to pass" but to get an answer and to decide who decides.
5. **Structural bonus.** Stages 1–3 are *qualification tests* (protect / present / pay).
   Stages 4–6 are *a preview of marriage* (compare / always on call / chores / obey). That mirrors
   the certificate — front: six passes; back: endless demands — and the reveal sits exactly on
   the seam.
6. **Final-fight stake.** With this arc the stake is the stage title 〈誰說了算〉. I withdraw my
   earlier "no rescue at stake" concern.
7. **Fits the brief:** "The player may notice clues before the hero does." The stage 1–2 tells
   serve exactly that.

**If adopted, these sections of `idea/storyboard/idea.md` would need edits (Claude's list):**
overview paragraph (lines 24–28); S1-03 and S1-04; S2-04; S3-01, S3-03, S3-04; S4-04 (小雨's
video call); V-01; the foreshadowing-table rows for 「不要傷到他」 and the roster check; and the
bullet 「主角會知道女友安全」 (around line 869). **S1-02 (the first prototype slice) is not
affected.**

**Risks:** (a) why he keeps going must be *shown*, not told; (b) tonal whiplash between the
satirical stages 1–3 and the darker 4–5 — keep the absurd comedy in 4–5 while his irritation rises.

## T-03 Stage 4–5 beat structure / 第四、五關的節奏

**Status / 狀態:** Leaning (depends on T-02)

- **Vincent:** If 阿安 knows from stage 3, the demands in stages 4–5 make more sense, and he
  has no need to answer so many times. 4–5: unreasonable demands; 阿安 becomes more and more
  unsatisfied. / 阿安若從第三關起就知情，第四、五關的不合理要求更說得通，他不需要一再回應；
  他只會越來越不滿。
- **Claude:** Agree. Change the repeated beat *demand → articulate reply → fight → stamp →
  不甘心* into *demand → a shrinking reply → the fight as his outlet → a grudging stamp*. Let his
  voice change by stage, so the dissatisfaction is carried by behaviour and not by speeches:
  / 同意。把重複的「要求 → 條理回應 → 打鬥 → 蓋章 → 不甘心」改成「要求 → 越來越短的回應 →
  打鬥成為出口 → 勉強蓋章」。讓他的語氣隨關卡改變，用行為而不是長篇對白表達不滿：

  | Stage / 關 | 阿安's voice / 阿安的語氣 |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | Urgent, shouting / 急迫、喊話 |
  | 2 | Puzzled, sarcastic / 困惑、帶刺 |
  | 3 | Hurt, deciding / 受傷、在做決定 |
  | 4 | Weary, few words / 疲倦、話少 |
  | 5 | Quiet, clear / 安靜、清楚 |
  | 6 | Firm / 堅定 |

  In stage 4 the reply can shrink across the three sisters: a full sentence for 雅婷, two short
  lines for 婉晴, and 「我知道。我先喝完。」 (already in the draft) for 可欣. Stage 5 needs no
  argument at all — the father's warning carries it. / 第四關三姊妹的回應可逐位縮短：雅婷一整句、
  婉晴兩句短話、可欣就是稿中已有的「我知道。我先喝完。」。第五關完全不需要爭論，由父親的警告承擔。

  而不只是一路把台詞縮短：**第四關是他停止討好；第五關是他看見繼續討好的代價。**

  - **雅婷：還想解釋，發現比較永遠沒有盡頭。** 留一次簡短回應即可，例如「我不是他」。
    之後她再換比較對象，他不再逐一辯解，把注意力轉回眼前的出口與對手。
  - **婉晴：發現回答甚麼都會被改成錯。** 「先說哪一件。」已足夠；她再改口，
    他停下同時接所有差事的動作。戰鬥保留她呼喚、換位與假動作的個性，
    不再在戰後補一段完整的相處原則。
  - **可欣：用行動留下自己的時間。** 看見小雨的訊息後，不急著替她找理由，也不與可欣辯論。
    打完後保留「我知道。我先喝完。」讓喝完那杯水成為第四關的收尾。
    S4-04 可以直接由「我們家都是這樣，爸爸也是」接到視訊裡父親被責罵，省去另一輪問答。
  - **建國：節奏放慢，讓同情與責任同時存在。** 保留阿安那句「你知道這樣不好，
    為甚麼還讓我走到這裡？」這是值得說完整的一句。父親的警告不應取代他的回答，
    也不能抹去他繼續攔路的事實。庭院交手後，接 S5-04 椅子被放回、父親再次低頭，
    讓阿安親眼看見剛才的警告如何發生。

  阿安的話少，應表現在他不再急著交出對方滿意的答案；保留他核對紀錄、收回承諾、
  喝水及等父親重新站好的主動行為。打鬥仍有突破攔阻的場景理由，不必把每一場都寫成憤怒宣洩。
  以上可用既有動作與短對白呈現，先不新增家務小遊戲或隱藏情緒數值。
