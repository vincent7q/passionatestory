# Option C — 「劇本亂了」 *Off Script*

> One of three storyboard options. Comparison and what all three share:
> [`story-v4-options.md`](story-v4-options.md). Comment anywhere with a new line starting `> 💬`.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A boy chases the van that took his girlfriend. Halfway through the family's staged kidnapping, **three real, very incompetent car thieves steal the van with her in it.** The family can't break character, so for the rest of the night they keep "attacking" him while secretly helping him catch the real ones. He notices none of it. |
| **The big surprise** | It comes in **two parts.** Mid-game (Stage 3) the plan breaks: the player sees the Lins panic for the first time. At the end the candidate, having just learned that everything was fake, points at the thieves: "They were actors too, right?" 林建國: **「那個是真的。」** |
| **Tone** | Farce. A heist movie told from the wrong side, with a family desperately trying to stay in character. |
| **When the player catches on** | Gradually through Stages 1–2, then **all at once in Stage 3** when the thieves drive off and 林媽媽 drops her credit card. From then on the player is in on the joke. The candidate never is. |
| **Closest to** | v3's original stinger, 「那個是真的」, turned into the middle of the plot instead of a coda. |

### What is actually happening

Same family, same test. But tonight three small-time car thieves, **阿龍, 阿虎 and their leader 豹哥**
(names placeholder), have picked the Lin family's garage to rob. They're hiding between the luxury
cars when the black van rolls in with its engine running and the keys in it. They take the van.
**They don't know there's a girl in the back.**

小雨 is not scared. This is the fourth time she's been kidnapped and the first time by amateurs. By
the end of Stage 4 the thieves are more afraid of her than of anyone chasing them.

The family can't tell the candidate, because the test is still running, so they switch to
**「計畫B」 (Plan B):** every scripted enemy still "fights" him, but in ways that push him toward the
van.

## Stage 2 — The metro 「捷運」 — 17:35 *(new)*

> **The family's question:** is he decent to strangers when he's in a hurry?

He fights from the back of the train to the front, carriage by carriage, while the van runs
alongside on the expressway outside the windows.

**S2-01 — Rush-hour carriage.** **Commuters who offer him their seat** before they attack:
> 乘客:「請坐。」 *(Please, sit.)*
Sitting refills 氣 and costs time. Standing for a pregnant woman / elderly man prop is a quiet 勇
moment with no prompt.

**S2-02 — School-trip carriage.** A class of kids on a field trip. They are **not enemies.** He has
to cross the carriage without hitting any of them (勇). The teacher counts heads as he passes.

**S2-03 — The announcement.**
> 廣播:「下一站:林家山。Next station: Lin Family Mountain.」
Every ad in the carriage shows the same smiling family. The candidate reads it as gang propaganda.

**S2-04 — Last carriage (boss): 大表哥 HOWARD.** Parkour on the hand-poles, swinging kicks, a
backflip off the luggage rack. 「不好意思,我史丹佛的。」 He's never rude, only humble-bragging.
- Defeated: he sits and offers the candidate his metro card. 「你很不錯。」
- **Exit:** the train pulls into 林家山 station. Below, he sees the black van turn into a garage
  at the foot of the mountain.

## Stage 3 — The garage 「車庫」 — 18:00 *(the plan breaks)*

> **The mother's question:** can he give her a future, and can he be bought?

**S3-01 — The garage.** A motor pool at the foot of the private mountain: luxury cars lit like a
showroom, plates starting with 林, and the van in the middle **with the engine running.**
- Clue: three pairs of trainers are visible under a Bentley.

**S3-02 — Boss: the Driver (林媽媽).** As in v3: Card Swipe, Designer Bags, Key Fob alarms,
Reversing, and 「月薪多少?」.

**S3-03 — One million.** At 30% she opens the suitcase. 「辛苦了。這裡一百萬。拿著,離開她。」
Press E → **E0**. He refuses, and she closes the case:
> 林媽媽:「……去吧。菜要涼了。」

**S3-04 — 「……我的車呢?」 *(…Where's my van?)*** She steps aside. They both turn around. **The
van is gone.** Its tail-lights are going up the mountain road, and three pairs of trainers are no
longer under the Bentley.

For the first time in the game, a Lin **loses her composure**. She drops her black card. Then,
calmly:
> 林媽媽:「……上車。」 *(…Get in.)*
She pulls the cover off a convertible.
> 他:「他們自己人內鬨了!有另一個幫派!」 *(They've turned on each other! There's a rival gang!)*

**The player now knows the truth, and the candidate has just invented an even bigger conspiracy.**

## Stage 4 — Up the mountain 「山路追擊」 — 18:20 *(new: vehicle stage)*

> **The family's question:** when it's real, does he still do it right?

A rolling stage up the private mountain road in the River City Girls style: **fights on top of
moving vehicles**, jumping lane to lane. 林媽媽 drives the convertible underneath him.

**S4-01 — Plan B.** The road is full of family staff who were *scripted* to fight him. They've just
been told over their earpieces. Subtitles show what they whisper to each other, but never to him:
> 園丁 *(into his sleeve)*:「計畫B!計畫B!」 *(Plan B! Plan B!)*
Their attacks now **help him**. A groundskeeper's "uppercut" launches him onto the next catering
truck: 「我、我要攻擊你了!往那邊!」 *(I'm, um, attacking you! That way!)* A guard dog "chases" him
toward the van. **The candidate thinks they are terrible at their jobs.**

**S4-02 — The van in sight.** Through the van's back window: 小雨, sitting very straight, talking.
The thieves look miserable. Snatches of her lines show in a speech bubble each time he gets close:
> 小雨:「安全帶。」 *(Seatbelt.)* / 「靠右開。」 *(Keep right.)* / 「你們有駕照嗎?」 *(Do you even
> have a licence?)*
> 阿虎:「大哥……我們放了她吧……」 *(Boss… let's just let her go…)*

**S4-03 — The tea table (boss): 豹哥, with 二叔 BAN.** 二叔's folding table is in the middle of the
road, exactly as planned. **The stolen van has to stop.** 二叔 beams at the thieves:
> 二叔:「坐。喝茶。」 *(Sit. Have some tea.)*
Nobody refuses an uncle, not even a criminal. **豹哥 sits down.** Then the candidate arrives, and the
fight is three-way chaos:

| Who | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **豹哥** (real) | Tracksuit, gold chain, a tyre iron that only ever hits with a cartoon *boing*. Every cup 二叔 serves him makes him slower |
| **二叔** | Serves **everyone**. Accepting cups still works for the candidate. Bowing still works too |
| **The trick** | Bow to 二叔 → he smiles, and as he steps aside **he "accidentally" tips the tea table onto 豹哥.** It's the only time 二叔 ever takes a side |

- 豹哥 is down, but 阿龍 and 阿虎 floor it and drive the van up the mountain. **The road only goes one
  place: the Lin estate.**

## Stage 5 — The estate 「城堡」 — 18:40

> **The family's question:** in chaos, who does he protect first?

**S5-01 — Courtyard.** The stolen van has **crashed into the fountain.** The thieves drag 小雨
inside the house. She tells them to wipe their feet, and they do.
- The seating chart has his name on it. He reads it as a hit list.

**S5-02 — Kitchen and halls: hospitality as a weapon.** The aunts and kitchen staff were scripted
to fight him. Now they have to fight him **and** stop two real thieves without breaking character,
so they do it the only way they know: **they feed the thieves.**
- Every time a thief passes an aunt, he is sat down and served a bowl of soup. Then a second. By the
  third bowl he can't stand up. **Rule 1 in its purest form: the family defeats criminals with
  hospitality.**
- **The toddler** takes down 阿虎 with one push. He sits down, rubs his head, and does not get up.
- The tureen tips toward the toddler: shield her (勇).

**S5-03 — The door.** He kicks it down.

### The dining room — final boss: 林建國

**D-01** The music stops. The table is set for eight, **plus three extra chairs.** In them sit the
three thieves, **napkins tucked in, bowls in front of them.** 小雨 sits beside her father.
林建國 has invited the criminals to dinner.
> 阿龍 *(mouthing to the candidate)*:「救我們。」 *(Help us.)*

**D-02** 林建國 puts down his chopsticks: 「我等你很久了。」 **The test continues as scheduled.**

**D-03 — Phase 1 「面談」.** Seated, chopsticks, questions. His summons now includes **the
thieves**, whom he waves into the fight: 「你們。去。」 *(You. Go.)* They go, because they're at
his table. Helped-up relatives still refuse to come.

**D-04 — Phase 2 「站起來」.** He stands. The dish flies at 小雨, and he shields her (勇). The bow.

## The reveal

**R-01 — Nothing happens.** He wins. Nobody runs.

**R-02** 小雨:「你來得好慢。」「……但你來了。」

**R-03 — Everyone bows,** in a line. At the end of it, **the three thieves bow too**, because
everyone else is bowing and they don't know what else to do.

**R-04** 林媽媽 takes off her sunglasses: 「車是我開的。」 Then, with a look at the thieves:
「……前半段。」 *(…The first half.)*

**R-05 — The form.** 力, 錢, 時, then the hidden **勇**: 「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是——你敢不敢。」
- **C-only stamp:** in the margin of the form, a red seal: **「真」 *(REAL)*.** *Rescued a real
  hostage.* No candidate in the family's history has one. (It isn't points. It's a mark.)

**R-06 — 「那個是真的。」** He finally understands that the van, the guards, the aunts and the tea
were all staged. He laughs, relieved, and points at the thieves:
> 他:「他們也是演的吧?」 *(They were actors too, right?)*
三叔 Ken walks in with three pairs of handcuffs.
> 林建國:「那個是真的。」 *(That one was real.)*
> 小雨:「中間那段是真的。」 *(The middle part was real.)*
**He faints.** He sees stars and sits down, cartoon style. An aunt brings him soup.

## Endings

**E0 — 「一百萬」.** He takes the money at the garage, and the thieves take the van *anyway*. He walks
down the mountain with the suitcase. Behind him, the credits are interrupted by the van flying past
in the opposite direction, 小雨's voice from inside: 「靠右開。」 Last shot: 林媽媽 calling her
husband: 「……親愛的,我們有兩個問題。」 *(…Darling, we have two problems.)*

**E1 — Approved.** The fruit plate is slid across. If the melon survived, it's the melon.
> 林建國:「你被批准了。下週日再來。」
Then, quietly, and only in C:
> 林建國:「一九九四年,沒有人真的被抓走。」 *(In 1994, nobody was really taken.)*「你比我強。」
> *(You did better than I did.)*

**E2 — 「重考」 *(Retake)*.** He failed on points but brought her back for real. 林建國 points at his 71:
> 林建國:「分數不夠。」 *(The score isn't enough.)* *(he taps the 「真」 seal)*「但這個,我沒有。」
> *(But this, I never had.)*「下週日再來。」

**E3 — Late.** He arrives after the thieves have eaten his dinner. **The three of them are full,
happy, and handcuffed.** 小雨:「我朋友問我為什麼還在等。」

**Post-credits — 「第2次」 *(Visit #2)*.** Next Sunday. A new prologue, a new street, the same
black van. The door slides open and **the three men in black are 阿龍, 阿虎 and 豹哥**, now in Lin
uniforms with a small 林 on the chest. 豹哥 holds the door open: 「請上車。」 *(Please, get in.)*
The family hired them.

## Why teenagers will like it

- **A mid-game twist.** Most games save the surprise for the end. Here it hits at Stage 3 and changes
  how the second half plays. Once players are in on the joke, every "attack" from the family is
  funny.
- **Kidnappers terrified of their hostage** is an easy, broad laugh, and it makes 小雨 the coolest
  person in the game.
- **The vehicle stage** gives gameplay variety and spectacle that River City Girls players expect.
- **「他們也是演的吧?」 → 「那個是真的。」** is the biggest single laugh line of all three options.

## Cost and risks

- **Cost: high.** New: the metro stage, a **vehicle / moving-platform stage** (new tech), a third
  faction (thieves fighting with or against everyone), "Plan B" enemy behaviour (AI that helps while
  looking hostile), and dinner with extra guests.
- **Risk: the purity of the test.** The family's test is interrupted by something real. That's the
  point (bravery is proven for real), but it means the second half is less about manners and more
  about action. The 勇 moments still have to be there (school-trip carriage, toddler, 小雨).
- **Risk: rule 2, nobody acknowledges the fighting is strange.** The family whisper 「計畫B」 to
  *each other*, never to him. That's within the rule, but it is the furthest any option bends it.
- **Rule 6, cartoon violence:** the thieves must be harmless clowns. A tyre iron that goes *boing*,
  nobody hurt, and in the end they're fed and then hired.
- **Spoiler check:** 勇 still appears first at R-05. The 「真」 seal is a seal, not a criterion.
