## 1. The concept

### Logline

A young man chases the black van that took his girlfriend across a city, up a private mountain and
into a mansion — never realising that every enemy is her family, every fight is a question, and the
kidnapping is a job interview for the man who will protect her and give her a future for the rest
of her life.

### The family's question

小雨 has been kidnapped for real three times — at 7, at 11 and at 14. None of it reached the
news: **the family paid a great deal to make it disappear.**

**Each time, it was her father who came for her.** He is fifty-four now.

So, as parents, they have two questions for any man who wants to marry her:

> **「如果有一天真的出事了,你敢不敢站在她前面?」**
> *If something really happens one day — will you dare to stand in front of her?*
> **「往後的日子,你能不能給她一個未來?」**
> *And in the years after — can you give her a future?*

They will not take his word for it. The only way to find out is to make him believe it is
happening.

### Five layers of truth

| Layer | When | What the player learns |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prologue | *She's been taken. Chase the van.* |
| 2 | Stage 2 | *Something is off.* Tea, apologies, 林 on every uniform |
| 3 | The reveal | *It was a test. This is her family. That was dinner.* Her mother was driving |
| 4 | **The Last Question** | *The test isn't over.* Real kidnappers — and he can't tell what's real any more. **Will he still step forward?** |
| 5 | The endings | *What really happened in 1994* — and what the father has been waiting for |

### Theme

> **To protect her is to dare to stand in front of her. To give her a future is to be able to
> hold up a home. Parents don't want a hero — they want someone they can rely on.**

### The father's real reason (revealed only at the end)

林建國 is not looking for a man stronger than him. He is looking for the man who can take over.
Three times he was the one who came for her, and he is getting old.

## 2. Characters

### The Lin family

**林小雨 CLORIS — 21, university student.** Survived three kidnappings. In the prologue it is she
who asks the question the whole game answers: 「如果有一天我被抓走了,你會來找我嗎?」 *(If I got
taken one day, would you come find me?)*

**林建國 VINCENT — 54, her father. Final boss.** Sat this test in 1994 and scored **71**. Fights
seated, with chopsticks, until he stands up. Kind, unreadable, never rude. *(Layer 5: in 1994, 71
was a fail. See E2.)*

**林媽媽 (name to decide — placeholder 沈雅芳) — 52, her mother. Stage 3 boss and the examiner.**
The test is her mother's (外婆's) rule; she brought it into the family. **She drove the black van
herself**, because she trusts no one else with her daughter. The father watches whether he
*dares*; **the mother watches whether he can give her daughter a future** — and whether he can be
bought. She signs the form.

**二叔 BAN — the tea uncle. Stage 2 boss.** Cannot be beaten by fighting. Offers three cups.
Respects a bow.

**三叔 KEN — a traffic police officer.** Real police. In Stage 1 he is directing traffic at a
junction and points the candidate the way: 「我們已經在處理了,你先追。」 *(We're on it. You keep
chasing.)* At the Last Question, he makes the real arrest.

**水果店老闆娘 — 小雨's aunt. Stage 1 boss. Not briefed.** Her stall is real and so is her anger.

**The rest of the family:** aunts who ask about grades, the Stanford cousin, the cousin doing
homework, the kitchen staff, the toddler 小表妹, and the staff with a small 林 on the chest.

**外婆 — the grandmother (seen only in a portrait).** Wrote the rule. Tested 林建國 in 1994.

### The candidate (the player)

**There is one candidate — the player.** 小雨's boyfriend. At the start the player chooses his
**look and fighting style**:

## 3. What the family measures

### The form — four lines

| Line | Shown during play? | Measures | Who cares most | Points (proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **力 POWER** | Yes — the green bar, which is also health | Can he protect her? | Father | 25 |
| **錢 MONEY** | Yes — the coin counter. *Every coin came off the family's own staff* | Can he hold up a home? Does he waste and wreck? | Mother | 15 |
| **時 TIME** | Yes — the clock, counting to 19:00. **Never labelled** | Dinner can't be late. Is he reliable? | Everyone | 20 |
| **勇 BRAVE** | **Never** — first seen on the form | Does he dare to stand in front of others? To say no to money? Does he pick on people who are down? | Father | 40 |
| **The Last Question** | Never — not a number | Pass / fail stamp: when he believes nothing is real, will he still step forward? | — | — |

Total 100. **Pass mark (proposed) 80** — so the father's 71 in 1994 really was a fail.

- ☐ No, shown on screen like 力 and 錢

### How 勇 is earned

Bravery is not hitting hard. **Picking on someone who is down is not brave.** Bravery is
stepping forward when it costs you something.

| Moment | Where | 勇 |
|---|---|---|
| Help a defeated opponent up within ten seconds — stopping mid-fight is a risk | All stages | +2 each |
| Never hit someone who is down | Whole run | +6 |
| Spare the fruit stall — don't touch the melon; protect an innocent | Stage 1 boss | +6 |
| **Refuse 林媽媽's million** | Stage 3 boss | +8 |
| **Shield the toddler** when the soup tureen falls | Stage 4 | +8 |
| **Shield 小雨** when the lazy Susan throws a dish at her | Final boss | +8 |
| Hit someone who is down | Any time | −3 each |
| Hit the toddler | Stage 4 | −5 |

The set pieces total **36, below the 40 cap** — full marks need some people helped up.

### 錢 and 時 in detail

- **錢:** defeated opponents drop coins. But **smashing a market stall gets you billed** — the
  owner hands over a bill and the money comes straight off the counter. A man who can hold up a
  home doesn't wreck things.
- **時:** reach the dining room before 19:00 for full marks; late loses points; very late scores 0
  and limits the ending (see §5). The clock is never explained — he thinks it's a rescue
  countdown.
- 二叔's tea table: fighting him can't win and only wastes time; accepting three cups and bowing is
  the fastest way through.
- 林建國's 「吃飽了嗎?」: force-feeding restores 力, **but eating takes time, and the clock keeps
  running.**

### Why a player who only fights can't pass

A pure masher: high 力 and 時, but smashed stalls (錢 billed), hit people who were down (勇
lost), never shielded anyone. Estimate: 力 25 + 錢 ~10 + 時 20 + 勇 ~8 (only for refusing the
million) ≈ **63** — **below 71, and well below the pass mark of 80.**

### Prologue

**P-01 — After class.** 17:00, a quiet street near campus, dusk.
He walks 小雨 home. She is quieter than usual. Out of nowhere she asks:
> 小雨:「如果有一天我被抓走了,你會來找我嗎?」 *(If I got taken one day, would you come find me?)*

**P-03 — Knocked flat.** He charges; one of the men puts him down gently — almost carefully —
and steps over him. **His phone hits the ground; the screen shatters.** *(Clue: "Sorry,"
muttered.)*

**P-04 — The hair clip.** The van is gone. On the ground: her hair clip. He picks it up and runs
after the van. *(Setup → E1.)*

### Stage 1 — City 「追」 *(The Chase)* — 17:10

> **The family's question: will he chase — and will he wreck things or hurt the innocent on the
> way?**

**S1-01 — Market Row.** Rush hour. Stalls everywhere. Office workers, a scalper, a delivery
rider block his path.
- Test: the stalls. Nothing says not to smash them — **but smashing one gets you billed (錢).**
- Clue: an office worker says 「不好意思」 *(excuse me)* while swinging his briefcase.

**S1-02 — The officer at the junction.** A traffic officer is directing cars. Before the
candidate can say a word, the officer points ahead:
> 三叔 Ken:「我們已經在處理了,你先追。」 *(We're on it. You keep chasing.)*
He thinks: *"Even the police are counting on me."*

**S1-03 — Covered Arcade, rain.** Rain drains 力 outside cover.
- Clue: a man glances at a slip of paper in his hand before attacking.

**S1-04 — Loading Bay.** Ambush from both sides. The first real test of the ten-second window:
defeated men sit down, stars circling, and wait.

**S1-05 — The fruit stall (boss).** 小雨's aunt. **Nobody told her.** A lunatic is running
through her melons, and she defends her stall with one.
- Test: **the melon.** Hit it and he loses 力, and the family notes it.
- Spare it and win → she points up the mountain road, the way the van went. (勇 +6)
- Destroy it → she sits down in the wreckage. Nothing is said. The file records it.

### Stage 2 — Mountain road 「山路」 — 17:35

> **The family's question: does he know when *not* to fight?**

**S2-01 — Lower Gate.** A private road, three kilometres, up a mountain — **the whole mountain
belongs to the Lins.** Groundskeepers, guard dogs.
- Clue: a small 林 embroidered on every uniform. The dogs wear collars.

**S2-02 — Pond Path.** Koi pond (falling in costs 力). Garden lights switch on **ahead** of him,
as if someone is expecting him.
- Clue: a defeated guard is helped up by another guard.

**S2-03 — Stone Steps.** A boy of nine throws stationery at him without looking up from his
homework. Estate Security: guards that block and wait — the first enemy that punishes mashing.

**S2-04 — The tea table (boss).** 二叔, enormous and beaming, sits in the middle of the road behind
a folding table with a full tea set.
- Knock him down → he pours another cup and stands up, fully restored. **Fighting can't win; it
  only wastes time.**
- Three times he offers a cup (E). Each one accepted refills 氣 and makes the candidate heavier.
  Refusing costs 力 — you do not refuse an uncle.
- After the third cup: **bow** (E). He steps aside, beaming: 「好孩子。」 *(Good kid.)*
- **This is where the player starts to understand. The candidate doesn't.**

### Stage 3 — The garage 「司機」 *(The Driver)* (new) — 18:10

> **The mother's question: can he give my daughter a future — and can he be bought?**

**S3-01 — The garage.** The top of the mountain. The mansion's underground garage — a row of
luxury cars lit like a showroom. In the middle: **the black van**, engine still running. He's
caught it. The van is empty — *"they must have taken her inside!"*
- Clue: every number plate starts with 林.

**S3-02 — The Driver (boss).** The driver's door opens. A woman in a cap and sunglasses steps
out without a word. (**林媽媽.** He has never met her and doesn't recognise her.)
All her moves are about money and the future:

| Move | Effect |
|---|---|
| 刷卡 Card Swipe | A fast slash with a black credit card |
| 名牌購物袋 Designer Bags | Heavy shopping bags swung in a wide arc |
| 遙控鑰匙 Key Fob | She presses the fob — every luxury car's alarm goes off and the doors pop open as obstacles |
| 倒車 Reversing | She jumps into the van and reverses across the garage (beeping; jump or change lanes) |
| 「月薪多少?」「存款呢?」 | Ranged questions: *"What's your salary?" "Savings?"* |

**S3-03 — One million (new).** At 30% health she stops. She slides open the van's side door,
takes out a suitcase and opens it — **full of cash.**
> 林媽媽:「辛苦了。這裡一百萬。拿著,離開她。」 *(Thank you for your trouble. One million. Take

The familiar **E** appears. All game, E has been the right button — help up, accept tea, bow.
**This is the only time pressing E is a trap.**
- **Press E, take it →** ending **E0 "One Million"** (the game ends here; see §5).
- **Refuse** (attack her, walk away, or press nothing for ten seconds) → she closes the case and
  steps aside:
  > 林媽媽:「……去吧。菜要涼了。」 *(…Go on. The food's getting cold.)* (勇 +8; a blatant clue)

### The dining room — final boss

**D-01 — The music stops.** A round table set for eight. Everyone turns to look at him.
小雨 is at the table, sitting very straight, hands in her lap, watching only him.
Beside her, a woman wears sunglasses indoors — a cap on the table in front of her.
- **On time:** the food is still steaming; everyone has waited for him.
- **Late / very late:** see arrival in §5.

**D-02 — 「我等你很久了。」** At the head of the table, a man in his fifties puts down his
chopsticks:
> 林建國:「我等你很久了。」 *(I've been waiting a long time for you.)*
A villain's line, a host's line, a father's line. He has already decided which. He attacks.

**D-03 — Phase 1 「面談」 *(The Interview)*.** The father fights **seated**, with chopsticks. The
family keeps eating; dishes are passed around the fight.
- His attacks are questions: 「你幾歲?」「有房子嗎?」 *(How old are you? Do you own a home?)*
  「吃飽了嗎?」 is a grab that force-feeds him — restores 力, **but the clock keeps running.**
- Every twenty seconds he calls two relatives to the fight. **Anyone he helped up tonight refuses
  to come.**

**D-04 — Phase 2 「站起來」 *(He Stands)*.** At 40% the father stands for the first time. The music
drops to a single drum. The family puts down their chopsticks.
- The lazy Susan spins to full speed. **A dish flies toward 小雨.** If he shields her, he takes the
  hit and loses his opening. (勇 +8)
- The way through: **bow** at the right moment. It staggers him.

### The reveal

**R-01 — Nothing happens.** He wins. He stands in the wreck of a dining room, breathing hard.
Nobody runs. Nobody calls for help. Hold on this.

**R-02 — 「你來得好慢。」** 小雨 stands up.
> 小雨:「你來得好慢。」 *(You took ages.)*
> *(quietly)*「……但你來了。」 *(…But you came.)*

**R-03 — Everyone bows.** Everyone he fought tonight files in behind him, dusts themselves off,
lines up and **bows**: the three men from the van, the office workers, 二叔 still holding the tea
set, 三叔 Ken in his police uniform. Then the fruit shop aunt bows, walks past him and sits down at
the table — still angry about the melon (or, if it survived, almost smiling).

**R-04 — 「車是我開的。」** The woman in sunglasses takes them off.
> 林媽媽:「車是我開的。」 *(I drove.)*
The driver he fought in the garage is 小雨's mother.

**R-05 — The form.** 林建國 slides a form across the table, his name already on it.
**力**, **錢** and **時** write themselves in, one by one — all good. A note beside 錢:
「這些是我們的錢。」 *(That's our money.)* He has a second to feel pleased.
Then the fourth line writes itself in. He has never seen it. It weighs more than all the others.
> 林建國:「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是——你敢不敢。」 *(Strength and money — anyone has those.

### The Last Question — the real final test

**L-01 — The door.** The doors crash open again. Three men in black — different men. They are
fast, and they grab 小雨.

**L-02 — Nobody moves.** This time **the family doesn't move.** Nobody helps. The music is gone.
The father looks only at him. **No prompt appears.** The player has control back.
- He — and the player — have just learned that nothing tonight was real. Is this?
- The player gets the same **ten seconds** the whole game taught them to count.

**L-03a — He goes.** He fights them. They are stronger than anyone tonight. Nobody helps. When
it's over, 三叔 Ken walks in with handcuffs.
> 林建國 *(writing on the last line)*:「那個是真的。」 *(That one was real.)*
> *(to him)*:「你還是來了。」 *(You came anyway.)*

**L-03b — He doesn't.** He shakes his head, smiling: 「別鬧了。」 *(Nice try.)* He sits down. The ten
seconds pass. The family moves — 林媽媽 takes down two men in four seconds without leaving her
chair. 小雨 does not look at him.
> 林建國 *(writing on the last line)*:「那個是真的。」 *(That one was real.)*

- ☐ **B. Never confirmed.** The player is left to wonder.

## 5. Endings

Four things decide the ending: **the score** (pass / fail), **the Last Question** (went / didn't),
**arrival time**, and **whether he took the million**.

| | Went (L-03a) | Didn't (L-03b) |
|---|---|---|
| **Pass, and not very late** | **E1 「下週日再來」 Approved** | **E3 「那個是真的」** |
| **Fail, or very late** | **E2 「重考」 Retake** — the 1994 truth | **E3 「那個是真的」** |

Plus: **took the million → E0 「一百萬」** (ends in Stage 3).

**E0 — 「一百萬」 *(One Million)*. The unexpected early ending.** He walks down the private
mountain with the suitcase. Night. The credits start to roll. Last shot: in the garage, 林媽媽
takes off her sunglasses and says into her phone:
> 林媽媽:「這是我們家花過最划算的一百萬。」 *(Best million this family ever spent.)*

**E1 — 「下週日再來」 *(Come back next Sunday)*.** The father slides him a plate of cut fruit
without looking at him — the highest honour in the game. *If the melon survived, it is the melon.*
He gives 小雨 back her hair clip. She takes it: 「我故意掉的。」 *(I dropped it on purpose.)*
> 林建國:「你被批准了。下週日再來。」 *(You're approved. Come back next Sunday.)*

**E2 — 「重考」 *(Retake)*. The unexpected one.** The score isn't enough (or he was very late). The
father is quiet for a long time. Then he points at his own line: 71.
> 林建國:「一九九四年,我七十一分。不及格。」 *(1994. I scored 71. I failed.)*
> 「但是那天晚上,真的有人來了。」 *(But that night, someone really came.)*
> 林媽媽:「他是唯一一個衝過來的。」 *(He was the only one who ran toward them.)*
> 林建國:「下週日再來。重考。」 *(Come back next Sunday. Retake.)* — if very late, he adds:
> 「早一點。」 *(Earlier.)*
*The number the player chased all game was a failing grade. What got the father in was the Last
Question.*

**E3 — 「那個是真的」 *(That one was real)*.** When it was real, he sat down. The family is
perfectly polite. The dinner ends. 小雨 doesn't look up. The stamp on the last line: 不合格 *(not
accepted)*.
- High-score version: **the most painful ending is the one with the best score.**
- Low-score version: the family pays for his taxi home; the fruit shop aunt hands him the broken
  melon to take with him.
