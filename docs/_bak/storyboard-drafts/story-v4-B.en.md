### What is actually happening

In **1994**, 外婆 staged a kidnapping of her daughter 雅芳 (now 林媽媽). Two young men ran the
course that night:

- **林建國** scored **71**.
- **陳先生**, the candidate's father, scored **70**. He lost one point at the fruit stall: he
  smashed the melons. The girl working the stall that night was a teenage 二姑, and she has not
  forgotten.

林建國 married 雅芳. That same night, the waitress carrying fruit out to the losing candidate was a
caterer's daughter, and she is now the candidate's mom. That's how his parents met.

Thirty years later their children met at university, entirely by chance. When the families worked
out who was who, **the two dads agreed on a rematch, through their sons.** His mom asked to be a
boss, and his dad asked to sit at the tea table. The two moms have had their own group chat for
eight months.

## Prologue

**P-01 — At home, 16:30.** His living room. His mom is ironing a shirt he's never seen before.
> 媽媽:「今天穿這件。」 *(Wear this one today.)*
His dad looks up from his newspaper, which he never does:
> 爸爸:「今天要見小雨?……加油。」 *(Seeing 小雨 today? …Good luck.)*
- Clues on replay: dad's knuckles are taped. A small trophy on the shelf reads **「1994 · 第二名」**
  (second place).

**P-02 — After class, 17:00.** He walks 小雨 home.
> 小雨:「如果有一天我被抓走了,你會來找我嗎?」 / 他:「誰會抓你啦。」

**P-03 — The van.** Three men in black and a driver in a cap and sunglasses. 小雨 is taken.

**P-04 — Knocked flat.** He's put down gently. His phone shatters. Her hair clip is on the ground.
He runs.

## Stage 1 — City 「追」 — 17:10

> **The family's question:** will he chase without wrecking the street?

**S1-01 — Market row.** Office workers who say 「不好意思」. A scalper. Delivery riders.

**S1-02 — The junction.** 三叔 Ken: 「我們已經在處理了,你先追。」

**S1-03 — Loading bay.** An ambush from both sides. The ten-second help-up window is taught here.

**S1-04 — The fruit stall (boss): 二姑.** Not briefed. She defends her melons. **Halfway through
the fight, she squints at him:**
> 二姑:「……你長得好像一個人。」 *(…You look just like someone.)*
- **Test: the melon.** In 1994 his father smashed it. **Spare it → she pauses, then points up the
  road:** 「……你比較乖。」 *(…You're better behaved.)* The player has no way to know yet what she
  means.
- Smash it → she sits down in the wreckage: 「……又來了。」 *(…Not again.)*

## Stage 2 — Campus at night 「校園」 — 17:35 *(new)*

> **The family's question:** is he the same person at school as at home?

The van cuts through his own university after dark. Every club is still in.

**S2-01 — The sports field.** The basketball team fires volleys of basketballs. The cheer squad
builds a human pyramid that collapses onto a lane (cartoon).

**S2-02 — Club Street.** **Club recruiters** grab him and push flyers into his hands (a stun with
the line: 「來參加我們社團!」 *Join our club!*). Defeated, they sit down and fan themselves with
flyers.

**S2-03 — The library.** **The library auntie** says 「噓——」, a shush that cuts his specials and
music for three seconds. The whole stage goes quiet, and so do the footsteps.

**S2-04 — The cafeteria (boss): 便當阿姨, the Lunch Lady.** Surgical mask, hairnet, apron. **It is his
mom.** He has no idea. The player will know within five seconds.

| Move | Effect |
|---|---|
| 拖鞋飛彈 **Flying Slipper** | Homing. Unblockable. Every Asian kid's oldest fear |
| 「外套穿好!」 *Zip your jacket!* | A grab that zips his jacket to the chin. He moves slower (too warm) |
| 「吃飽沒?」 *Have you eaten?* | Force-feeds him. Restores 力, costs time. **It mirrors 林建國's move in Stage 5** |
| 「房間整理了沒?」 *Did you clean your room?* | A question stun |
| **全名攻擊 The Full Name** | At 30% she shouts **his full name.** It fills the screen as a shockwave. The scariest attack in any household |

- When defeated she sits down and rubs her head. If he helps her up, **she straightens his collar**,
  presses a lunchbox into his hands (his favourite, which restores 力) and disappears. The lunchbox
  has a note: 「趁熱吃。」 *(Eat it while it's hot.)*
  > 他:「……這阿姨人真好。」 *(…What a nice lady.)*
- If he hits her while she is down, she just looks at him. No words. It costs a lot.

## Stage 3 — Mountain road 「山路」 — 18:00

> **The family's question:** does he know when *not* to fight, and does he respect his elders?

**S3-01 — S3-03.** As in v3: the private mountain, 林 on the uniforms, collared dogs, lights
coming on ahead, and the boy doing homework who throws stationery.

**S3-04 — The tea table (tag-team boss): 二叔 BAN + the Straw-Hat Uncle.** Two old men at a folding
table in the middle of the road. 二叔 pours tea. Across from him, a man in a straw hat and an
obviously fake beard is playing **象棋 (Chinese chess)**. **It is his dad.**

| Who | Moves |
|---|---|
| **二叔** | Three cups (E). Accept: 氣 up, heavier. Refuse: 力 down. Can't be beaten by force |
| **Straw-Hat Uncle** | 車 *Chariot* charge (a straight line). 炮 *Cannon* lob (a piece hops over and lands). 「將軍!」 *Checkmate!*, a grab that pins him to the board |
| **Both** | While he fights one, the other comments. Dad: 「手放低。」 *(Lower your hands.)* **It is the exact tip dad gave him at age eight.** A flashback panel flickers for half a second |

- **Way through:** accept three cups, then **bow to both of them.**
- The Straw-Hat Uncle stands aside, and his beard slips. Under his breath:
  > 草帽伯伯:「……長大了。」 *(…You've grown up.)*
  > 他 *(bowing politely)*:「謝謝伯伯!」 *(Thank you, sir!)*

## Stage 5 — The estate 「城堡」 — 18:40

**S5-01 — Courtyard.** Catering vans and a seating chart. **This time the chart has his name, and
two more: 「陳先生 · 陳太太」.**
> 他:「他們連我爸媽都抓了!」 *(They've got my parents too!)*
It's the best misreading in the game. He runs faster.

**S5-02 — Ancestral hall.** A photo on the wall: **1994. Two young men at this same table, both
holding forms.** One is 林建國. The other has his back to the camera.

**S5-03 — Kitchen, three waves, the toddler and the tureen.** As in v3. The Stanford cousin mentions
Stanford. Twice.

**S5-04 — The door.** He kicks it down.

### The dining room — final boss: 林建國

**D-01** The music stops. A round table set for **ten**. **Two chairs are empty.** A straw hat hangs
on the coat rack, and a hairnet is folded on one of the empty chairs.

**D-02** 「我等你很久了。」

**D-03 — Phase 1 「面談」.** Seated, chopsticks, questions. 「吃飽了嗎?」 is a force-feed, the
same move as the lunch lady's 「吃飽沒?」. Summons relatives, and those he helped up refuse.

**D-04 — Phase 2 「站起來」.** At 40% he stands. A dish flies at 小雨; shield her (勇). Bow to stagger
him. As 林建國 staggers, **a voice from the kitchen doorway shouts 「手放低!」** He lowers his
hands without thinking, and the final hit lands.

## The reveal

**R-01 — Nothing happens.** He wins. Nobody runs.

**R-02** 小雨:「你來得好慢。」「……但你來了。」

**R-03 — Everyone bows.** All of them: the men from the van, the club recruiters (still holding
flyers), the cheer squad (in pyramid formation), 二叔.

**R-04 — The lunch lady.** She takes off the mask. **His mom.**
> 媽媽:「兒子!你剛剛那個迴旋踢,媽媽都錄起來了!已經傳到家族群組了!」 *(Son! I filmed your
> spinning kick! It's already in the family group chat!)*
He cannot speak.

**R-05 — The Straw-Hat Uncle.** He takes off the beard. **His dad.**
> 爸爸:「……腳步還是太亂。」 *(…Your footwork's still a mess.)*

**R-06 — The Driver.** 林媽媽 takes off her sunglasses: 「車是我開的。」

**R-07 — The form.** 力, 錢 and 時 come in, then the hidden fourth line, **勇**:
「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是——你敢不敢。」
Under his total there are **two** printed lines:

```
        林建國    (1994)   71
        陳XX      (1994)   70
```

Silence. Everyone looks at his dad.
> 林建國:「差一分。」 *(One point.)*
> 爸爸:「那是因為西瓜。」 *(That was the melon.)*
> 二姑 *(from the doorway)*:「對。」 *(Yes.)*

**R-08 — How they met.** His mom puts a hand on his dad's arm:
> 媽媽:「那天晚上,是我端水果給你爸的。」 *(That night, I was the one who brought your father the
> fruit.)*
The father of the girl he loves and his own father have been rivals for thirty years. **Tonight was
the rematch, and he was the player.**

## Endings

**E0 — 「一百萬」.** He takes the money. In the garage, 林媽媽 makes a phone call:
> 林媽媽:「……他拿了。」 *(…He took it.)*
> 媽媽 *(on speaker)*:「拖鞋準備好了。」 *(I've got the slipper ready.)*

**E1 — Approved.** His score decides **which father wins tonight:**
- **Above 71:** 林建國 is quiet, then: 「……你比我們兩個都強。」 *(…You're better than both of us.)*
  His dad stands up, knocks his chair over, and shouts 「我兒子!」 *(That's my son!)*, thirty years of
  losing by one point gone in one second.
- **70–71:** 林建國 and his dad look at each other. 「……平手。」 *(…A draw.)* Both dads, at the same
  time: 「下週日再比。」 *(Rematch next Sunday.)*
- Either way: the plate of cut fruit is slid across, and **his mom slides a second plate next to
  it.** 小雨 takes back her hair clip: 「我故意掉的。」
  > 林建國:「你被批准了。下週日再來。」

**E2 — 「重考」 *(Retake)*.** He failed, below 70. 林建國 looks at the son, then the father.
> 林建國:「下週日再來。」 *(to his dad)*「你也一起。」 *(You too.)*
Over the credits: **a father-and-son training montage.** Shadow-boxing in the park, running stairs,
dad yelling 「手放低!」, and mom filming all of it for the group chat.

**E3 — Late.** Both families have been waiting. The food is cold.
> 小雨:「我朋友問我為什麼還在等。」
> 媽媽 *(quietly, to his dad)*:「……跟你一樣。」 *(…Just like you.)*

**Secret — 「一九九四」 *(1994)*, a playable bonus stage.** Unlocked by beating 71. You play
**young dad** in 1994: CRT filter, older pixel art, a pager instead of a phone, the same fruit stall
with a teenage 二姑.
- **If you smash the melon:** history plays out as it did. 70. It ends on the fruit plate and a young
  waitress smiling.
- **If you spare the melon:** he scores 71 and ties 林建國. The screen glitches, and a photo of the
  candidate and 小雨 **starts to fade** (a *Back to the Future* gag). A caption appears:
  **「歷史不能改。」** *(History can't be changed.)* The tape rewinds and the melon is on the stall
  again.

## Why teenagers will like it

- **The parent humiliation is universal.** The flying slipper, the full-name shout, "zip up your
  jacket", and mom filming your fight and posting it to the family chat. Teenagers will recognise
  every one of these and cringe-laugh.
- **The reveal hits twice:** "it was her family" (expected by now) and then "it was *my* family too"
  (not expected). The soap-opera 1994 line lands as a third twist.
- **It has heart.** Under the jokes, a son finishes the thing his dad lost by one point.
- **The playable 1994 bonus** is a reason to replay, and the "history can't be changed" glitch is
  very shareable.

## Cost and risks

- **Cost: medium.** New: the campus stage (four enemy types), the lunch-lady boss, a tag-team boss,
  and a reveal with three unmaskings. **The 1994 bonus stage is optional**: it reuses Stage 1 with
  a filter and a different stall owner sprite.
- **Risk: "he doesn't recognise his own mom."** It is a comedy convention (Clark Kent's glasses).
  It works because the player is in on it, and it fits rule 5, since he never suspects. Her mask and
  hairnet need to be strong enough that his line 「這阿姨人真好」 is funny, not stupid.
- **Risk: the 1994 rivalry.** Two young men courting the same woman thirty years ago has to stay
  light. Both dads are happily married now and the rivalry is about the *score*, not about her.
- **Spoiler check:** nothing names 勇 before R-07. The trophy says 第二名, never the criterion.
