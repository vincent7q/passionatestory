## What all three keep (from your v2/v3 comments)

- **One candidate, the player.** Felix / Lucian / Hilman are a look and a fighting style.
- **Five stages**, each with a boss, and the fifth ends in the dining room against **林建國**.
- **林媽媽 is a boss**: the van driver in the garage, and the one-million suitcase is the only time
  **E** is a trap (ending E0).
- His **phone breaks** in the prologue, and 三叔 **KEN** points the way.
- **Dinner at 19:00.** Time is scored and changes the ending.
- Scoring as in v3 §3: 力 POWER, 錢 MONEY and the clock are on screen; **勇 BRAVE is hidden until
  the form.** Stalls you smash get billed against 錢. The masher estimate (~63, under 71) is
  unchanged because the hidden-column structure is the same.
- **The wealth is shown big**: a private mountain, a garage of luxury cars, a million in cash,
  plus one new show-off per option (a drone, a metro station named after the family).
- The six content rules: nobody rude, nobody remarks on the fighting, every enemy likes him, **he
  never suspects**, cartoon violence only.
- The beloved beats: 二叔's three cups, the melon, 「我等你很久了」, the father fighting seated, 「車是我開的」,
  the cut fruit plate.

## What none of them include

- **v3's "Last Question"** (real kidnappers after the reveal). A and B drop it. C turns that idea
  into the main plot. Any of them can take it back if you want it.
- The old "funny stinger" (removed per your v2 comment H2).

## My recommendation

**B**, with one graft from A:

- B has the strongest surprise for teenagers (your *own* parents were in on it) and the strongest
  heart (the son finishes what his dad lost by one point). Its jokes (the flying slipper, the
  full-name shout, mom filming and posting the fight) need no explanation to any teenager.
- Graft **A's E1 ending** into B: getting **added to the family group chat** fits B naturally, since
  his mom is already posting to it.
- If budget allows later, **C's "aunts defeat criminals with soup"** works as a bonus gag in any
  version.
