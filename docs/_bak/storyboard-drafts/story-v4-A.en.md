## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | A boy chases the van that took his girlfriend through a city, a night market, up a private mountain, into a garage and a mansion. Eighty-seven relatives watch the whole thing live from a drone and argue about him in the family group chat. |
| **The big surprise** | At the reveal the TV shows the group chat: a whole evening of his worst and best moments, with stickers, polls and a three-minute voice message from grandma. **In the good ending he gets added to the chat.** |
| **Tone** | Sitcom. The clues build slowly and it all pays off in one big reveal. |
| **When the player catches on** | Stage 2–3 (Howard's selfie, the drone, the tea). The candidate never does. |
| **Closest to** | v3, so it carries the least risk. Most v3 beats survive, plus two new stages' worth of gags. |

### What is actually happening

It is the same setup as v3. 小雨 has been kidnapped for real three times, the family paid to keep it
all out of the news, and now any man who wants to marry her goes through a staged kidnapping.

The new part in A is that **the family watches.** The nine-year-old cousin, 小樂, flies a drone above
the candidate all evening while he does his homework, and the feed goes to 「林家相親相愛一家人」, the
family LINE group with 87 members. Aunts post reactions, uncles start polls, and grandma sends voice
messages. Each family member reads their cue in the chat just before they step out to fight him.

One aunt is not in on it. The fruit shop aunt never reads the group, which is why she was never
briefed.

## Prologue — 17:00

**P-01 — Bubble tea.** A tea shop near campus. 小雨 orders for both of them, and she already knows
his order (半糖少冰). She types something on her phone and smiles. Then, out of nowhere:
> 小雨:「如果有一天我被抓走了,你會來找我嗎?」 *(If I got taken one day, would you come find me?)*
> 他:「誰會抓你啦。」 *(Who'd want to take you?)*

**P-02 — The van.** A black van. Three men in black. One of them **holds the door open for her**.
She gets in, or is pulled in. She looks straight at him. The driver wears a cap and sunglasses.

**P-03 — Knocked flat.** He charges, and one man puts him down gently. His phone hits the pavement
and the screen shatters. The man puts the bubble tea back in his hand:
> 黑衣人:「你的飲料。」 *(Your drink.)*

**P-04 — The drone.** The van is gone. Her hair clip is on the ground. He grabs it and runs. As he
does, **a small drone lifts off a rooftop and follows him.** It stays in the sky, somewhere on
screen, for the rest of the game. Nobody mentions it.

Title card: **熱血物語:見家長**.

## Stage 1 — City 「追」 *(The Chase)* — 17:10

> **The family's question:** will he chase without wrecking the street?

**S1-01 — Shopping street.** The shopping street at rush hour.
- **Street dancers** are mid-battle in the middle of the road. They attack on the beat. **If he
  stands still for three seconds, they read it as a freeze, applaud, and leave.** This is the only
  enemy you beat by doing nothing.
- Office workers swing their briefcases and say 「不好意思」.

**S1-02 — The junction.** 三叔 Ken is directing traffic. One whistle and **every car stops at the
same moment.**
> 三叔 Ken:「我們已經在處理了,你先追。」 *(We're on it. You keep chasing.)*

**S1-03 — Delivery riders.** Riders with insulated bags. One bag splits open and the receipt says
**「林公館 · 19:00 · 8人」**. He doesn't read it. This is where the **ten-second help-up window**
is taught: a defeated rider sits down with stars circling and waits.

**S1-04 — The fruit stall (boss): 二姑.** She was not briefed, so as far as she knows a lunatic is
running through her melons.

| Move | Effect |
|---|---|
| 滾西瓜 Melon Bowling | Melons roll across the lanes |
| 榴槤 Durian Toss | A spiky arc. Cartoon *bonk* |
| 挑水果 Ripeness Check | A grab. She squeezes his arm like fruit: 「太軟。」 *(Too soft.)* |

- **Test: the melon.** Spare it → she points up the road (勇). Smash a stall → she hands him a
  bill and it comes straight off 錢.
- **Exit:** the van's tyre marks lead into the night market.

## Stage 2 — Night market 「夜市」 — 17:35 *(new)*

> **The family's question:** when everything is loud, fun and free, does he keep his head?

**S2-01 — Entrance.** Smoke, lights, and free samples everywhere. **Sampling refills 氣 but the clock
keeps running** (a 時 temptation). A cloud of stinky tofu blocks one lane as an area hazard.

**S2-02 — Games alley.** Barkers throw ring-toss hoops that pin him in place. A child's **goldfish
bag** gets knocked into the air: catch it (E) before it lands (勇). No prompt says the bag matters.

**S2-03 — Claw machine arcade.** One enemy type pops out of a claw machine and the claw grabs him.
- Clue: every plush prize is a little bear with **林** stitched on its chest.

**S2-04 — Boss: 大表哥 HOWARD, the Stanford cousin.** He fights on the ring-toss stage. He is never
rude. He is relentlessly humble about being wonderful.

| Move | Effect |
|---|---|
| 「不好意思,我史丹佛的。」 | A taunt that buffs him. He uses it constantly |
| 射氣球 Dart Volley | A fan of balloon darts |
| 彈珠台 Pinball | Launches him across the stage like a pinball |
| 自拍 Selfie | A flash stun. **He posts the photo.** You see the phone screen for one frame: `(87)` |

- When defeated he sits down, rubs his head, and says: 「你很不錯。……我史丹佛的。」
- If helped up, he gives the candidate a shuttle-bus ticket to the mountain. The bus is private and
  the logo on the ticket is 林.

## Stage 3 — Mountain road 「山路」 — 18:00

> **The family's question:** does he know when *not* to fight?

**S3-01 — Lower gate.** The road is private and so is the mountain. Groundskeepers have 林 on their
uniforms.
- **Guard dogs** charge him, but press E and he pets them. A petted dog rolls over and stays out of
  the fight. (It isn't scored. It is just nice.)

**S3-02 — Pond path.** Garden lights switch on **ahead** of him. The drone is lower now, low enough
to read the **林** on its side.

**S3-03 — Stone steps.** 小樂 (9) sits at a folding desk doing his homework. He throws stationery
with one hand. **The other hand is on a drone controller.**
> 小樂:「我在寫功課。」 *(I'm doing homework.)*

**S3-04 — The tea table (boss): 二叔 BAN.** The same as v3, which works:
- Knock him down and he pours another cup and gets up fully restored. Fighting only wastes time.
- Three cups (E). Each one refills 氣 and makes the candidate heavier. Refusing costs 力.
- Then a bow (E): 「好孩子。」
- **New for A:** every cup comes out of a different, tinier and more golden tin. He never says what
  it costs. Between cups he checks his phone, laughs at something, and puts it away.

## Stage 4 — The garage 「司機」 *(The Driver)* — 18:25

> **The mother's question:** can he give her a future, and can he be bought?

**S4-01 — The garage.** A row of luxury cars lit like a showroom. The black van is in the middle with
the engine running. Every plate starts with 林.
- Clue: the phone on the van's dashboard lights up nonstop. **`林家相親相愛一家人 (87) · 999+`**

**S4-02 — Boss: the Driver (林媽媽).** Cap and sunglasses. She doesn't say a word until she starts
asking questions.

| Move | Effect |
|---|---|
| 刷卡 Card Swipe | A fast slash with a black card |
| 名牌購物袋 Designer Bags | A wide, heavy swing |
| 遙控鑰匙 Key Fob | Every car alarm goes off and the doors pop open as obstacles |
| 倒車 Reversing | She reverses the van across the garage, beeping |
| 「月薪多少?」「存款呢?」 | Ranged questions: *Salary? Savings?* |

**S4-03 — One million.** At 30% she stops, opens a suitcase full of cash and says:
> 林媽媽:「辛苦了。這裡一百萬。拿著,離開她。」 *(Thank you for your trouble. One million. Take it,
> and leave her.)*

**E** appears. **This is the only time E is a trap.** Press it → ending **E0**. Refuse → she
closes the case: 「……去吧。菜要涼了。」 (勇)

## Stage 5 — The estate 「城堡」 — 18:40

> **The family's question:** in chaos, who does he protect first?

**S5-01 — Courtyard.** Catering vans, a helipad, and a rolled-up banner being hoisted.
- **A seating chart on an easel, with his name on it.** He reads it as a hit list.

**S5-02 — The mahjong room (mini-boss): 麻將阿姨團.** Four aunties at a spinning mahjong table. Each
call is an attack:

| Call | Attack |
|---|---|
| 「碰!」 | Three tiles thrown in a line |
| 「槓!」 | Four tiles, a wall he has to jump |
| 「幾公分?」「哪間大學?」 | Question stuns |
| 「胡了!」 | The table flips over into a screen-wide tile rain. Cartoon |

**S5-03 — Kitchen.** Woks, warming trays, and somebody tuning a piano. The toddler wanders into the
fight. **A soup tureen tips toward her.** If he steps in front of it, he gets splashed instead (勇).
No prompt.

**S5-04 — The door.** The music swells. He kicks the door down mid-roll.

### The dining room — final boss: 林建國

**D-01** The music stops. A round table set for eight, and everyone turns. Everyone at the table
**has a phone face-down beside their bowl.**

**D-02** 「我等你很久了。」 He attacks.

**D-03 — Phase 1 「面談」 (The Interview).** The father stays seated with his chopsticks while the
family keeps eating. His attacks are questions: 「你幾歲?」「有房子嗎?」. 「吃飽了嗎?」 force-feeds
him, which restores 力 while the clock keeps running. **Every few seconds a phone on the table
buzzes.** Every 20 s he summons two relatives, and **anyone the candidate helped up tonight refuses
to come.**

**D-04 — Phase 2 「站起來」 (He Stands).** At 40% he stands. Everyone **turns their phones face-up**
to watch. The lazy Susan spins and a dish flies toward 小雨; shield her (勇). Bow to stagger him.

## The reveal

**R-01 — Nothing happens.** He wins. Nobody runs.

**R-02** 小雨:「你來得好慢。」 *(quietly)*「……但你來了。」

**R-03 — Everyone bows.** Everyone he fought tonight files in. The street dancers bow **in sync**,
Howard bows and takes a selfie during it, and 二叔 bows holding the tea tray.

**R-04** 林媽媽 takes off her sunglasses: 「車是我開的。」 *(I drove.)*

**R-05 — The form.** 力, 錢 and 時 write themselves in, all good. Then the fourth line, which he
has never seen: **勇.**
> 林建國:「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是——你敢不敢。」

**R-06 — The group chat.** 小雨 hands him his phone, already repaired. The dining room TV turns on.
**「林家相親相愛一家人 (87)」** scrolls back through the evening, with drone clips attached:

> **17:01** 小雨:「他會來的。」
> **17:12** 二姑:「有個瘋子在踩我的西瓜!!!」
> **17:12** 林媽媽:「……那就是他。」
> **17:13** 二姑:「什麼?」
> **17:40** Howard: *(selfie, mid-combo)*「我史丹佛的。」
> **17:58** 三叔 Ken *(poll)*:「讓他上山? 👍 64 👎 23」
> **18:10** 外婆:🎤 *voice message, 2:47*
> **18:33** 林媽媽:「他沒拿。」
> **18:58** 林建國:「上菜。」

**The chat is built from his actual run.** Each message is triggered by something he did. If he
smashed the stall, 二姑's message is angrier. If he petted every dog, there's a dog photo. If he
hit someone who was down, the clip is there too, followed by one aunt's 「唉。」 (Data-driven: each
message is a `StoryEvent` with a condition. Nothing appears that he didn't do.)

**R-07 — The album.** One more tap: an album titled **「小雨的男朋友(兩年)」**, two years of photos
of him. A café: 「他先付錢。」 A street, last spring: 「他以為沒人看到。」 The delivery rider from
Stage 1 waves, because he delivers the candidate's food every week.

## Endings

Arrival time, the score and the million decide the ending (v3 rules). **There is no Last Question
in A.** It can be grafted on from v3 if you want it.

**E0 — 「一百萬」 *(One Million)*.** He walks down the mountain with the suitcase while the credits
roll. In the garage, 林媽媽 posts to the group:
> 林媽媽:「這是我們家花過最划算的一百萬。」 *(Best million this family ever spent.)*
> *(87 people react 👍.)*

**E1 — 「你已被加入群組」 *(You've been added)*.** He passes and is on time. The father slides over a
plate of cut fruit without looking at him, which is the highest honour in the game. If the melon
survived, the fruit is that melon.
> 林建國:「你被批准了。下週日再來。」
His phone buzzes:
> **「林小雨」已將你加入「林家相親相愛一家人」**
Then it buzzes **four hundred times.** It's an avalanche of good-morning stickers (it is 19:42).
The last one is from 外婆: a lotus flower that says 「早安」. **Cut to credits.**

**E2 — 「重考」 *(Retake)*.** He failed but came. The poll comes up: 「再給他一次機會?」 It is tied,
and the deciding vote is 林建國's. He points at his own 1994 line, **71**:
> 林建國:「一九九四年,我七十一分。不及格。」「下週日再來。重考。」

**E3 — Late.** The table has been cleared and the chat has moved on to someone's cat.
> 小雨:「我朋友問我為什麼還在等。」 / 林建國:「再說吧。」

**Secret — 「三十年後」 *(Thirty years later)*.** Unlocked by E1 with a full 勇 column. A round table
set for eight. A man in his fifties sits at the head and we only see his back. He types one message
into a group chat: 「上菜。」 The doors crash open and he puts down his chopsticks:
> 「我等你很久了。」

**Game over.** 力 hits 0 and he wakes on a sofa under a blanket, with a note: 「休息一下,再去。」
The drone is hovering outside the window.

## Why teenagers will like it

- **The group chat is real life.** Every teenager has a family chat full of good-morning stickers
  and polls nobody asked for. Getting added to it as the "reward" is a punchline they'll feel.
- The reveal is **personal**: it replays *their* run, *their* mistakes, with relatives reacting.
  That's very shareable.
- The night market and mahjong aunties are familiar places and people turned into boss fights.

## Cost and risks

- **Cost: low–medium.** It reuses most of v3. New: the night market stage, the Howard boss, the
  mahjong mini-boss, and the group-chat reveal UI (a scrolling list built from triggered events,
  which the event system needs anyway).
- **Risk:** the drone and buzzing phones are clues the player can read, so the reveal is
  satisfying rather than shocking. That's the classic version of the surprise. B and C go further.
- **Spoiler check:** the chat appears **after** the form, so 勇 never shows before R-05. During
  play the player sees only `(87)` and `999+`, never a message.
