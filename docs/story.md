# 熱血物語:見家長 — *Meeting the Parents*

**Selected story.** This is the narrative bible. `docs/PRD.md` covers mechanics; where the two
disagree about story, this file wins.

Chinese is Traditional, matching 熱血物語 in `docs/idea.md`.

---

## The two premises

This game has two, and the gap between them is the whole thing.

### What the candidate believes

After class, 林小雨 is walking with him when a black van pulls up. Three men in black drag her in.
He is knocked flat. The van is gone before he can stand.

He chases it across the city, up a mountain, and into a fortified estate, fighting through
everything in his way to get her back. He is genuinely afraid. He is genuinely in love. He does not
stop for twenty minutes.

### What is actually happening

The Lin family is extremely wealthy. 小雨 has survived three real kidnapping attempts — ages 7, 11,
and 14 — and none of them ever reached the news.

The family concluded long ago that whoever marries her must be able to handle the next one himself.
So they stage one.

Every man in the van is on the payroll. Every guard on the mountain is staff. The estate is not a
fortress, it is a house, and there is a dinner on the table getting cold.

Tonight three candidates are running the same course, an hour apart. None of them knows about the
others. The family did not consider this worth mentioning.

**He is not rescuing her. He is meeting her parents.**

---

## The engine

### Layer one — escalating absurdity that the hero refuses to see

The evidence mounts steadily and he never once registers it, because he has decided what is
happening and he is not a man who revises.

Enemies apologise while attacking. They wear matching uniforms with a small 林 embroidered on the
chest. One checks a clipboard between waves. A "bandit" halfway up the mountain blocks the path and
refuses to let him pass until he sits down and has tea. By the third stage there are catering vans
in the courtyard and a seating chart on an easel.

The player will start to suspect somewhere in stage 2. The candidate never does.

### Layer two — the metric he can't see

Here is the part that matters.

**The game scores him on two axes, and only tells him about one.**

He can see damage, combos, and clear times. What he cannot see is that the family is measuring
something else entirely: whether he stays decent while he believes the woman he loves is in mortal
danger.

Does he keep hitting a man who is already down? Does he wreck a stranger's fruit stall because it's
in his way? Does he stop — with every second counting — to help someone he just knocked over back
onto their feet?

Anyone can be polite at dinner. That tells you nothing. The Lins want to know who you are when you
think the stakes are real, and the only way to find out is to convince you that they are.

During play, this shows up as a gold `+3 禮` that pops occasionally with no explanation. Players will
assume it's a minor bonus. It is the entire test.

---

## The evaluation is on screen the whole time

Nothing on the HUD is disguised or renamed. It is the assessment, live, for twenty minutes, and
nobody looks at it properly because it is shaped exactly like a video game.

| On screen | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| 力 **POWER** — the green bar | 有本事嗎? Can he handle himself. Worth **40**. |
| 錢 **MONEY** — the counter | 有錢嗎? What he's worth. Worth **20**. |
| the clock, counting to 18:00 | Dinner. He is being scored on punctuality for a meal he doesn't know he's invited to. |
| 氣 SPIRIT | Just a meter. Not a criterion. Grandma doesn't care. |

Those are the two questions every family asks about a suitor, sitting in the corner of the screen in
a 48-point pixel font, and neither the candidate nor the player ever wonders why a *rescue* has a
power bar and a money counter. Because that is simply what games look like.

**力 doubles as health.** Getting beaten reduces your power, which is both mechanically ordinary and
exactly how the family sees it. Reaching zero is not death — he wakes somewhere safe, having been
carried there, and is told he can try again. Three attempts.

### And they are the two that don't decide it

力 and 錢 are worth sixty points between them, and a determined candidate maxes both.

The column he never sees is worth forty, and it is the one that separates him from 71.

> 「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是別的。」
> *(Strength and money — anyone has those. I was looking at something else.)*

The money is the sharpest part. Every coin he collected dropped off a man on the Lin payroll. It was
their money the entire time, he has been diligently gathering it all night, and it counted in his
favour. Grandma's note on that line reads 「這些是我們的錢。」 *(That's our money.)*

---

## Stage 1 — 城市「追」 / *The Chase*

**17:20.** The van is pulling away. He runs.

Through the commercial district at rush hour, over market stalls and delivery scooters, shoving
through a crowd that is very much in his way.

**Who he fights:** what he takes for the gang's street muscle. Office workers on their commute. A
delivery rider. Men who happen to be standing where he needs to be.

**The clues, still deniable:** one of them says 「不好意思」 while swinging. Another glances at
something in his hand before engaging. They go down easily and they go down *carefully*.

**Boss — 水果店老闆娘 (the Fruit Shop Owner).**

She is not part of the test. She is a real woman with a real fruit stall, and a lunatic is sprinting
through it. She blocks him, and she fights, because that is her livelihood on the ground.

He thinks she's with them. She isn't.

**The melon is the test.** She defends her stall with it, and it can be destroyed. He is desperate,
he is out of time, the van is getting away — and the correct move is to be careful with a stranger's
fruit. The family is watching to see whether he can do that.

This is the sharpest 禮 test in the game and it lands eleven minutes before he has any idea he's
being tested.

---

## Stage 2 — 森林「山路」 / *The Mountain Road*

**17:45.** The trail goes up a private mountain. There is one road and it is three kilometres long.

**Who he fights:** guards. Groundskeepers. Dogs. A boy of about nine who throws stationery at him
without looking up from his homework.

**The clues, now absurd:** every uniform has a small 林 on the chest. The dogs are well fed and
wearing collars. The "guards" apologise, help each other up, and are unfailingly polite. The estate
lights come on automatically as he passes, as though someone is expecting him.

**Boss — 二叔 (Second Uncle).**

An enormous, beaming man is sitting in the middle of the mountain road behind a folding table with
a full tea service laid out. He will not let the candidate past until he has sat down and had a cup.

He is not aggressive. He is *hospitable*, immovably, and he cannot be beaten by fighting — knock him
flat and he simply pours another cup and gets back up.

Three cups. Each one accepted restores 氣 and makes the candidate heavier and slower. Each one
refused costs 力, because you do not refuse an uncle. The third leaves him at his most sluggish,
and the way through is to bow.

He steps aside beaming and tells the candidate he's a good kid.

**This is the moment the player understands.** A kidnapper does not do this. The candidate takes it
as a bizarre obstacle and keeps running.

---

## Stage 3 — 城堡「城堡」 / *The Castle*

**18:00.** The estate. He is certain this is where they are keeping her.

**Who he fights:** what he reads as the inner guard. Aunts. Cousins. Kitchen staff. A woman with a
serving spoon who asks about his grades mid-combo. A cousin who mentions, twice, that he went to
Stanford. A toddler in the corner who cannot be hit and who hits him.

**The clues, now blatant and screamed at him:** catering vans in the courtyard. A seating chart on
an easel by the door. Warming trays. Someone is tuning a piano. A banner is being hung, and it is
not a threat.

He notices none of it. He is looking for a dungeon.

---

## The dining room

He kicks the last door down mid-roll, braced for the boss.

The music stops.

It is a **dining room.** A long table, set for eight, mid-meal. Everyone turns to look at him.

小雨 is at the table with a bowl in front of her, on her phone. Not restrained. Not frightened.
Mildly annoyed that he's late.

At the head of the table, a very small ninety-one-year-old woman puts down her chopsticks and says:

**「我等你很久了。」** *(I have been waiting a long time for you.)*

Which is a villain's line and a host's line, and he has already decided which one it is.

He attacks.

---

## Final boss — 奶奶 CLORIS 克洛麗絲

He believes he is fighting the mastermind. He is fighting a grandmother at her own dinner table, and
she is going to win on points.

Ninety-one. Four foot nine. Undefeated since 1958. She fights seated for the entire first phase and
does not raise her voice once.

**The rest of the family keeps eating.** Dishes are passed around the fight. An aunt asks someone to
move so she can reach the fish. Nobody is alarmed, because nobody here is in any danger, and the
candidate reads their calm as the arrogance of professionals.

Her opening moves are questions — 「你幾歲?」「有房子嗎?」— which he hears as taunting and which are
just the questions. 「吃飽了嗎?」 is an unblockable grab that force-feeds him: it restores his 力 and
quietly takes a bite out of a column he does not know exists.

At 40% she stands up for the first time. The music drops out entirely. The rest of the family
quietly puts down their chopsticks to watch.

The way through is to bow. She respects manners more than strength and always has.

---

## The reveal

He wins. He is standing in the wreckage of a dining room, breathing hard, and nothing happens next.

Nobody runs. Nobody calls for help.

**1.** 小雨 puts her phone down and says: 「你來得好慢。」 *(You took ages.)*

**2.** The men he defeated tonight file in behind him. All of them. They dust themselves off, line up,
and **bow** — including the three from the van, and the fruit shop owner, and Second Uncle, still
carrying the tea set and extremely pleased with himself.

**3.** Grandma slides a form across the table. His name is on it. It is mostly already filled in.

**4.** Three lines on it. The first two he has been staring at all night without once wondering why
they were there — **力** and **錢**. He has maxed them both and he is, briefly, quite pleased.

Then the third line, which he has never seen, and which is worth more than either.

**禮.** Every time he chose not to finish someone off. Every time he stopped to help someone up with
the clock running. The fruit stall he did or did not wreck.

「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是別的。」

**5.** The man sitting beside Grandma is 小雨's father. He scored **71** in 1994. He has been at this
table all evening watching. When the candidate looks at him, he shrugs.

**6.** Grandma turns the page.

The file does not start tonight. It starts the day they met.

> **PHOTO** — a café, two years ago
> 「他先付錢。」 *(He paid first.)*
>
> **PHOTO** — a street, last spring, him helping a stranger with a dropped bag
> 「他以為沒人看到。」 *(He thought no one was watching.)*

The delivery rider he fought in stage 1 is the man who brings his food every week. He waves.

---

## The stinger

Three men in black kick the door in. Real ones.

The candidate does not move. He has just learned that nothing tonight was real, and he cannot tell
anymore, and he is not going to be made a fool of twice.

The family destroys all three in about four seconds without leaving the table. An aunt does it
one-handed while asking whether anyone wants more soup. 小雨 does not look up.

Grandma writes something on the form. Then, to the candidate:

**「那個是真的。」** *(That one was real.)*

---

## Ending

She slides a plate of cut fruit toward him without making eye contact. It is the highest honour
available in this game.

Across the table, 小雨 mouths 「你贏了」 *(you won)*.

**「你被批准了。下週日再來。」** *(You have been approved. Come back next Sunday.)*

Post-credits: 「第2次」 *(Visit #2)*. It begins again, and it is harder, and he still doesn't know
what's coming.

Rule 47: Survive.

---

## The three candidates

All three love her. All three ran the same course tonight, an hour apart, and none of them knows the
others exist.

**FELIX 菲利克斯** — 21. *Balanced.*
Sincere to a fault, and the least perceptive man alive. Has a handwritten letter folded in his
jacket that he never gets a chance to give to anyone. His instinct is to simply be a good person,
which is a catastrophic combat strategy and an outstanding score on the axis he can't see.

*File note:* 「他不知道自己在被評估。這是重點。」 *(He doesn't know he's being evaluated. That's the
point.)*

**LUCIAN 盧西安** — 22. *Speed.*
Fast, evasive, allergic to a direct answer. His specials are all deflections — he wins exchanges by
not being where the attack landed. Filmed part of the chase on his phone, which he will regret.

*File note:* 「他問了三次她好不好。沒問過自己好不好。」 *(He asked three times if she was all right.
Never asked about himself.)*

**VINCENT 文森特** — 27. *Power.*
The adult. Treats the rescue as a professional operation — brings equipment, draws a map, works the
problem. Slow, enormous, devastating.

He is the only candidate who ever suspected. Somewhere on the mountain he wrote *"this feels staged"*
in his notebook and then talked himself out of it.

They searched his bag at the door. The note is in the file.

## Content rules

The comedy dies if any of these slip. They are not stylistic preferences.

1. **No character is ever rude.** Hostility is expressed entirely through hospitality.
2. **Nobody acknowledges that the fighting is strange** — not the family, not the staff, not the
   candidate.
3. **The wealth is shown, never stated.** No one says the family is rich.
4. **Every enemy has a reason to like you.** They fight you anyway.
5. **The candidate never suspects.** Not once, not for a frame. The player may; he may not.
6. **Cartoon violence only.** Nobody bleeds, nobody is hurt, nobody stays down. Defeated opponents
   see stars, sit down, and rub their head. This is a family game about a family.

## Not selected

Two other concepts were considered. Kept in case anything is worth grafting on later.

**A —「這是誤會啊!」** — Three boys destroy a city rescuing a girl from her own surprise birthday
party. Player knows from second ten; the heroes never work it out. Cloris was the event coordinator,
eight months of planning, rage meter labelled 「預算」. Ended on 「損失估計:NT$4,820,000」.
*Its property-damage counter is the idea worth stealing.*

**C —「英雄太多了」** — You run to save a girl; everyone who sees you running joins in; by sunset
4,000 people are rescuing her and the rumour has mutated until the crowd believes she *is* a wolf.
Cloris was a museum night guard — the only reasonable person in the game, framed as a raid boss.
