## 1. Comments on the original idea

### What works — keep it

- **The twist.** A desperate rescue that turns out to be meeting her parents. Funny, warm, and
  recognisable to anyone who has met a partner's family: 「你幾歲?」「有房子嗎?」「吃飽了嗎?」
- **The HUD is the evaluation, in plain sight.** 力 and 錢 sit on screen all game and nobody asks
  why a rescue has a money counter. Very clever.
- **The hidden third score**, revealed only on the form. The game's message is carried by the
  mechanics, not by a speech.
- **The ten-second window** to help a defeated opponent up. A real choice, under time pressure,
  that costs you something.
- **二叔's tea table** and **the father's 71 from 1994.** Memorable, and they give the family a
  history.

### What needs fixing — holes in the story

| # | Problem | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | **The theme doesn't match your goal.** You want the family to test *"can he protect her in the future"*. The current story tests *"is he decent under pressure"* (manners, restraint). Related, but not the same | The ending must answer the question the game asks |
| H2 | **The stinger works against the theme.** Real kidnappers arrive and the candidate **does nothing**, because he thinks it's fake. That is the moment he *fails* to protect her — but it's a cutscene, and the family saves the day | The most important test happens without the player |
| H3 | **小雨 is a prize, not a person.** She's on her phone, annoyed. She has survived three kidnappings and has no voice in the story | Players need to care about who they are protecting |
| H4 | **Whose tradition is it?** 林建國 "sat where the candidate sits" in 1994 — so in 1994 *he* was the suitor, tested by *his wife's* family. But 小雨's mother never appears | A plot hole, and a wasted character |
| H5 | **Three candidates, one girlfriend.** "All three love her" — is she dating three men at once? | Players may dislike her, or the premise |
| H6 | **Nobody calls the police.** A real person whose girlfriend is kidnapped calls 110 first | Players will ask |
| H7 | **The clock doesn't work.** Stage 3 starts at 18:00 and dinner is at 18:00, so he is always late | Breaks the "on time / late" endings |
| H8 | **Only one real ending** (approved, or a late variant). You want *unexpected endings* | Little reason to replay; no surprise after the reveal |

### What v2 changes

| Original | v2 | Fixes |
|---|---|---|
| The family tests his manners | The family asks one question: **"If someone really came for her one day — would you come? And what would you do?"** Every stage tests one part of protecting someone | H1 |
| The stinger is a cutscene | It becomes **The Last Question** — the final, playable test, *after* the reveal, when he believes nothing is real | H2, H8 |
| 小雨 is passive | She asks the question in the prologue, **texts him all game**, and has her own private question: *will he come?* | H3 |
| Mother absent | **林媽媽** keeps the tradition (it came from her own mother). She drove the van | H4 |
| Three boyfriends | All three **confessed** to her in the same week. She gave each the same answer: *"Meet my family first."* | H5 |
| No police | He does call. The officer who answers is **三叔** | H6 |
| Dinner at 18:00 | **Dinner at 19:00**; the stage times move | H7 |
| One ending | **Four endings + a secret ending**, decided by the score *and* the Last Question | H8 |

## 2. The concept

### Logline

A young man chases the van that took his girlfriend across a city, up a mountain and into a
fortress — never realising that every enemy is her family, every fight is a question, and the
kidnapping is a job interview for the man who will protect her for the rest of her life.

### The family's question

小雨 has been kidnapped for real three times — at 7, at 11 and at 14. None of it reached the news.

**Each time, it was her father who came for her.** He is fifty-four now.

So the family has one question for any man who wants her, and they will not take his word for the
answer:

> **「如果有一天,真的有人來帶走她——你會不會來?你會怎麼做?」**
> *If someone really came for her one day — would you come? What would you do?*

The only way to find out is to make him believe it is happening.

### Five layers of truth

The story is built so that the player keeps discovering the game was about something deeper.

| Layer | When | What the player learns |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prologue | *She's been kidnapped. Chase the van.* |
| 2 | Stage 2 | *Something is off.* Tea, apologies, the family name on every uniform |
| 3 | The reveal | *It was a test. This is her family. That was dinner.* |
| 4 | **The Last Question** | *The test isn't over.* Real kidnappers — and he no longer knows what's real. **Would you still protect her?** |
| 5 | The endings | *What 1994 really was* — and what the father has been waiting for |

### Theme

> **Protecting someone isn't winning a fight. It's coming for them — and not hurting anyone on
> the way.**

### The father's real reason (revealed only at the end)

林建國 is not looking for a man who is better than him. He is looking for the man who can take
over. He has been the one who comes for her three times, and he is getting old.

## 3. Characters

### The Lin family

**林小雨 CLORIS — 21, university student.** Survived three kidnappings. She **knows about the
test** and agreed to it on one condition: she sends the texts. Her private question is not the
family's — it is simply **「他會不會來?」** *(Will he come?)* Three times, only her father came.

**林建國 VINCENT — 54, her father. Final boss.** Sat this test in 1994 and scored **71**. Fights
seated, with chopsticks, until he stands up. Kind, unreadable, never rude. *(Layer 5: in 1994, 71
was **not** a pass. See E2.)*

**林媽媽 (name to decide — placeholder 沈雅芳) — 52, her mother. The examiner.** The test is her
mother's rule; she brought it into the family. **She drove the black van herself**, because she
does not trust anyone else with her daughter. At dinner she wears sunglasses indoors. She signs the
form — the father only fills it in.

**二叔 BAN — the tea uncle.** Cannot be beaten by fighting. Offers three cups. Respects a bow.

**三叔 — a traffic police officer.** Real police. Answers the candidate's 110 call: 「我們已經在
處理了,你先追。」 *(We're on it. You keep chasing.)* At the Last Question, he makes the real arrest.

**水果店老闆娘 — 小雨's aunt. Not briefed.** Her stall is real and so is her anger. Stage 1 boss.

**The rest of the family:** aunts who ask about grades, the Stanford cousin, the cousin doing
homework, the kitchen staff, the toddler 小表妹, the staff in uniforms with a small 林 on the
chest.

**外婆 — the grandmother (seen only in a portrait).** Wrote the rule. Tested 林建國 in 1994.

### The three candidates

All three confessed to 小雨 in the same week. She gave each the same answer: **「你要先見我家人。」**
*(You have to meet my family first.)* Tonight is that. They run the same course, an hour apart;
none knows the others exist.

| Candidate | Who he is | What the Last Question means for him |
|---|---|---|
| **FELIX 菲利克斯**, 21 — balanced | Sincere, the least perceptive man alive. Carries a handwritten letter he never gets to give her | He never suspects anything — so when it's real, he simply goes. *"He doesn't know he's being evaluated. That's the point."* |
| **LUCIAN 盧西安**, 22 — speed | Fast, evasive, never gives a straight answer. Films the chase on his phone | The Last Question is the first question he cannot dodge |
| **HILMAN 希爾曼**, 27 — power | The adult. Plans, maps, works the problem. The only one who suspects — writes *"this feels staged"* in his notebook on the mountain | He was right before. He's the most likely to sit this one out. **Can a man who suspects everything still act?** |

## 4. What the family measures

### The form

| Line | Shown during play? | Measures |
|---|---|---|
| **力 POWER** (40) | Yes — the green bar, which is also health | Can he handle himself? |
| **錢 MONEY** (20) | Yes — the coin counter. *Every coin came off the family's own staff.* | What is he worth? |
| **The hidden line** (40) | **Never** — first seen on the form | Kindness and protection: does he help up the people he knocks down, spare what isn't his, shield the weak, keep fighting away from her? |
| **最後一題 The Last Question** | Never — it isn't a number | Pass / fail stamp. Did he protect her when he believed nothing was real? |

**[DECISION D1] — the hidden line's character**
- ☐ **A. Keep 禮** (manners, propriety) — your original. 「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是別的。」
- ☐ **B. 心** (heart) — covers both kindness and protection. 「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是心。」 *(my
  recommendation: it fits the new theme, and the father's line lands harder)*
- ☐ **C. 護** (protect) — the most direct, but gives the theme away if anyone sees the character

Whichever is chosen, the rule stays: **it never appears on screen before the form.**

### What raises the hidden line

| Moment | Where |
|---|---|
| Help a defeated opponent up within ten seconds | All stages |
| Never hit someone who is down | All stages |
| Spare the fruit stall (don't touch the melon) | Stage 1 boss |
| Leave the market stalls standing | Stage 1 |
| Accept 二叔's three cups, then bow | Stage 2 boss |
| **Shield the toddler** when the tureen falls | Stage 3 (new) |
| Bow to 林建國 at the right moment | Final boss |
| **Shield 小雨** when the lazy Susan throws a dish at her | Final boss (new) |

Penalties: hitting someone who is down; hitting the toddler; being force-fed by 「吃飽了嗎?」.

He walks 小雨 home. She is quieter than usual. Out of nowhere she asks:

**P-05 — The text.** His phone buzzes. From 小雨's number: **「救我」** *(Help me.)* He runs.

### Stage 1 — City 「追」 *(The Chase)* — 17:10

> **The family's question: will he come — and will he hurt anyone on the way?**

**S1-01 — Market Row.** Rush hour. Stalls everywhere. Office workers, a scalper, a delivery
rider block his path.
- Test: the stalls. Nothing says not to smash them.
- Clue: an office worker says 「不好意思」 *(excuse me)* while swinging his briefcase.

**S1-02 — The 110 call.** He calls the police while running. The officer who answers is calm:
> 三叔:「我們已經在處理了,你先追。」 *(We're on it. You keep chasing.)*
The candidate: *"Even the police are counting on me."*

**S1-03 — Covered Arcade, rain.** Rain drains 力 outside cover.
- Clue: a man glances at a slip of paper in his hand before attacking.
- Text from 小雨: 「往山上。」 *(Up the mountain.)* He reads it as a brave secret message.

**S1-04 — Loading Bay.** Ambush from both sides. The first real test of the ten-second window:
defeated men sit down, stars circling, and wait.

**S1-05 — The fruit stall (boss).** 小雨's aunt. **Nobody told her.** A lunatic is running
through her melons, and she defends her stall with one.
- Test: **the melon.** Hit it and he loses 力, and the family notes it.
- Spare it and win → she points up the road, the way the van went.
- Destroy it → she sits down in the wreckage. Nothing is said. The file records it.

### Stage 2 — Mountain road 「山路」 — 17:40

> **The family's question: does he know when *not* to fight?**

**S2-01 — Lower Gate.** A private road, three kilometres, up a mountain. Groundskeepers, guard
dogs.
- Clue: a small 林 embroidered on every uniform. The dogs wear collars.

**S2-02 — Pond Path.** Koi pond (falling in costs 力). Garden lights switch on **ahead** of him,
as if someone is expecting him.
- Clue: a defeated guard is helped up by another guard.
- Text: 「山路很滑,小心。」 *(The road's slippery. Be careful.)* He reads it as a warning about
  traps.

**S2-03 — Stone Steps.** A boy of nine throws stationery at him without looking up from his
homework. Estate Security: guards that block and wait — the first enemy that punishes mashing.
- *Hilman only:* he stops and writes in his notebook: *"this feels staged."* Then crosses it out.

**S2-04 — The tea table (boss).** 二叔, enormous and beaming, sits in the middle of the road behind
a folding table with a full tea set.
- Knock him down → he pours another cup and stands up, fully restored. He **cannot** be beaten by
  fighting.
- Three times he offers a cup (E). Each one accepted refills 氣 and makes the candidate heavier.
  Refusing costs 力 — you do not refuse an uncle.
- After the third cup: **bow** (E). He steps aside, beaming: 「好孩子。」 *(Good kid.)*
- **This is where the player starts to understand. The candidate doesn't.**

> **The family's question: in chaos, who does he protect first?**

**S3-01 — Front Courtyard.** Catering vans. A banner being hung.
- Clue (blatant): **a seating chart on an easel — with his name on it.** He reads it as a hit

**S3-02 — Ancestral Hall.** Portraits on the walls. One photo: a young man at this same table,

**S3-03 — Kitchen.** Kitchen staff with woks. Warming trays. Someone is tuning a piano.

**S3-05 — The door.** A pair of carved doors. Music swells. He kicks them down mid-roll, braced

### The dining room

**D-01 — The music stops.** A round table set for eight, mid-meal. Everyone turns to look at him.
小雨 is at the table with a bowl in front of her, on her phone — **she was texting him all
along.** A woman beside her wears sunglasses indoors.

**D-02 — 「我等你很久了。」** At the head of the table, a man in his fifties puts down his
chopsticks:
> 林建國:「我等你很久了。」 *(I've been waiting a long time for you.)*
A villain's line, a host's line, a father's line. The candidate has already decided which. He
attacks.

**D-03 — Phase 1 「面談」 *(The Interview)*.** The father fights **seated**, with chopsticks. The
family keeps eating; dishes are passed around the fight.
- His attacks are questions: 「你幾歲?」「有房子嗎?」. 「吃飽了嗎?」 is a grab that force-feeds him
  (restores 力, quietly costs the hidden line).
- Every twenty seconds he calls two relatives to the fight. **Anyone the candidate helped up
  tonight refuses to come.** *(Payoff of every ten-second choice.)*

**D-04 — Phase 2 「站起來」 *(He Stands)*.** At 40% the father stands for the first time. The music
drops to a single drum. The family puts down their chopsticks.
- The lazy Susan spins to full speed. **A dish flies toward 小雨.** If the candidate shields her,
  he takes the hit and loses his opening. *(The second shield test.)*
- The way through: **bow** at the right moment. It staggers him.

### The reveal

**R-01 — Nothing happens.** He wins. He stands in the wreck of a dining room, breathing hard.
Nobody runs. Nobody calls for help. Hold on this.

**R-02 — 「你來得好慢。」** 小雨 puts down her phone.
> 小雨:「你來得好慢。」 *(You took ages.)*
> *(quietly)*「……但你來了。」 *(…But you came.)*

**R-03 — Everyone bows.** Everyone he fought tonight files in behind him, dusts themselves off,
lines up and **bows**: the three men from the van, the office workers, 二叔 still holding the tea
set, 三叔 in his police uniform. Then the fruit shop aunt bows, walks past him and sits down at the
table. She is still angry about the melon.

**R-04 — 「車是我開的。」** The woman in sunglasses takes them off.
> 林媽媽:「車是我開的。」 *(I drove.)*
She is 小雨's mother.

**R-05 — The form.** 林建國 slides a form across the table, his name already on it.
**力** writes itself in — good. **錢** — good, with a note: 「這些是我們的錢。」 *(That's our
money.)* He has a second to feel pleased.
Then the third line writes itself in. He has never seen it. It is worth more than either.
> 林建國:「力氣跟錢,誰都有。我看的是別的。」 *(Strength and money — anyone has those. I was
> looking at something else.)*

Underneath, already printed: **林建國 (1994) — 71.**

**R-06 — The file.** The file does not start tonight. It starts the day they met.
> PHOTO — a café, two years ago: 「他先付錢。」 *(He paid first.)*
> PHOTO — a street, last spring, helping a stranger with a dropped bag: 「他以為沒人看到。」 *(He
> thought no one was watching.)*
The delivery rider from Stage 1 waves: he brings the candidate's food every week.
*(Lucian only: his own phone video of the chase is in the file. 「傳給我們。」 *(Send us a
copy.)*)*

**R-07 — Relief.** Laughter. Someone pours him tea. 小雨 almost smiles. The bottom line of the
form is still blank. **The player believes the game is over.**

### The Last Question *(new — the real final test)*

**L-01 — The door.** The doors crash open again. Three men in black — different men. They are
fast, and they grab 小雨.

**L-02 — Nobody moves.** This time **the family doesn't move.** Nobody helps. The music is gone.
The father looks only at the candidate. **No prompt appears.** The player has control back.
- The candidate — and the player — have just learned that nothing tonight was real. Is this?
- The player gets the same **ten seconds** the whole game taught them to count.

**L-03a — He goes.** He fights them. They are stronger than anyone tonight. No one helps — except
小雨, who stamps on one man's foot at the right moment. When it's over, 三叔 walks in with
handcuffs.
> 林建國 *(writing on the last line)*:「那個是真的。」 *(That one was real.)*
> *(to the candidate)*:「你還是來了。」 *(You came anyway.)*

**L-03b — He doesn't.** He shakes his head, smiling: *"Nice try."* He sits down. The ten seconds
pass. The family moves — 林媽媽 takes down two men in four seconds without leaving her chair;
小雨 does not look at him.
> 林建國 *(writing on the last line)*:「那個是真的。」 *(That one was real.)*

**[DECISION D3] — were they real?**
- ☐ **A. Real.** 三叔 arrests them. Simple and serious. *(my recommendation)*
- ☐ **B. Never confirmed.** The family won't say. The player is left to wonder.
- ☐ **C. Staged too** — the family's most elaborate trick. *(Risk: it makes the lesson feel
  cheap.)*

### Endings

The ending is decided by **the score** (pass / fail) **and the Last Question** (went / didn't).
Arrival time (on time / late / very late) changes the table and the lines, never the score.

| | Went (L-03a) | Didn't (L-03b) |
|---|---|---|
| **Score passes** | **E1 「下週日再來」 Approved** | **E3 「那個是真的」** |
| **Score fails** | **E2 「重考」 Retake** — *the 1994 truth* | **E4 「謝謝你今天來」** |

**E1 — 「下週日再來」 *(Come back next Sunday)*.** The father slides him a plate of cut fruit
without looking at him — the highest honour in the game. *If the melon survived, it is the melon.*
He gives 小雨 back her hair clip. She takes it: 「我故意掉的。」 *(I dropped it on purpose.)*
> 林建國:「你被批准了。下週日再來。」 *(You're approved. Come back next Sunday.)*
*(Felix only: he finally gives her the letter.)*

**E2 — 「重考」 *(Retake)*. The unexpected one.** The score isn't enough. The father is quiet for a
long time. Then he points at his own line: 71.
> 林建國:「一九九四年,我七十一分。不及格。」 *(1994. I scored 71. I failed.)*
> 「但是那天晚上,真的有人來了。」 *(But that night, someone really came.)*
> 林媽媽:「他是唯一一個衝過來的。」 *(He was the only one who ran toward them.)*
> 林建國:「下週日再來。重考。」 *(Come back next Sunday. Retake.)*
*The number the player chased all game was a failing grade. What got the father in was the Last
Question.*

**E3 — 「那個是真的」 *(That one was real)*.** A high score. Perfect manners. And when it was real,
he sat down. The family is perfectly polite. The dinner ends. 小雨 doesn't look up. The stamp on
the last line: 不合格 *(not accepted)*. **The most painful ending is the one with the best
score.**

**E4 — 「謝謝你今天來」 *(Thank you for coming today)*.** A polite rejection. The family pays for
his taxi home. The fruit shop aunt hands him the broken melon to take with him.

**Arrival — changes the table, not the score:**
- *On time* — the family waits, the food is warm.
- *Late* — 林建國:「菜都涼了。」「你比我慢。」 *(The food's gone cold. You were slower than me.)*
- *Very late* — the table is cleared, someone is washing up. 小雨:「我朋友問我為什麼還在等。」
  *(My friends asked why I was still waiting.)*

**Post-credits (E1, E2) — 「第2次」 *(Visit #2)*.** Next Sunday. It begins again, harder.

**Secret ending — 「三十年後」 *(Thirty years later)*.** *Unlocked by reaching E1 with all three
candidates.*
A round table, set for eight. A man in his fifties sits at the head — we only see him from behind;
it could be any of the three. 小雨 sits beside him. Their daughter is on her phone.
The doors crash open. A young man rolls in, braced for a fight.
The man at the head of the table puts down his chopsticks.
> 「我等你很久了。」 *(I've been waiting a long time for you.)*

### Game over

**G-01 — Waking up.** 力 reaches zero. He wakes up somewhere safe — a sofa, a blanket over him —
carried there by someone. A note: 「休息一下,再去。」 *(Rest. Then go again.)* Three tries per
evening.
